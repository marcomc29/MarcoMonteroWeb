Profile & Background

The Artist

Composer and guitarist from San Ramón de Alajuela, Costa Rica. With a background shaped by exact sciences, biology, and studying electric guitar since age 14, he approaches music from an analytical and honest perspective.

He debuted in 2022 with the album New Adventures and solidified his evolution toward a more introspective musical language with the EP Entre Mareas (2024) and the LP Ecos de Anochecer (2025).
